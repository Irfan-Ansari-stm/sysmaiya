General Questions

Everything you need to know about working with TechNova Solutions.

What services does TechNova provide?
We provide web development, mobile app development, AI integration, cloud infrastructure, SaaS platforms, enterprise solutions, UI/UX design, and automation services.
How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. Small business websites may take 2–4 weeks, while enterprise solutions can take several months.
Do you offer custom pricing?
Yes. We create customized pricing plans based on your project requirements, features, integrations, and timelines.
Which technologies do you use?
We work with modern technologies such as React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, NestJS, PostgreSQL, AWS, Docker, AI APIs, and cloud platforms.
Do you provide post-launch support?
Absolutely. We offer maintenance, monitoring, updates, performance optimization, and ongoing technical support packages.
Can you help with AI and automation?
Yes. We specialize in AI-powered chatbots, workflow automation, intelligent business systems, and AI integrations tailored to your business.
