Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how TechNova Solutions uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience.

Last Updated: 07 May 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function efficiently, remember preferences, and improve user experiences.

2. Why We Use Cookies

TechNova Solutions uses cookies to:

  • Improve website performance and speed
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Enhance security and fraud prevention
  • Provide personalized user experiences

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Necessary for the website to function properly, including authentication, navigation, and security.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our website to improve performance and user experience.

Preference Cookies

Store user preferences such as language, theme settings, and form information.

Marketing Cookies

Used to provide relevant advertisements, campaign tracking, and personalized promotions.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party services such as analytics, cloud hosting, payment gateways, and marketing platforms that place cookies on your device.

These third parties are responsible for their own privacy and cookie practices.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. You may:

  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Allow only selected cookies
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality and user experience.

6. Cookie Consent

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy.

You can update your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

7. Updates to This Policy

TechNova Solutions may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page along with the updated revision date.
